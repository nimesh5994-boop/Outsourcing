PAYE Reconciliation

BrightPay payroll data vs the General Ledger already uploaded for this job (the Nominal Activity / General Ledger Detail section below — no separate upload needed here). Net Pay is reconciled employee-by-employee-by-month, since that's paid to each person individually; HMRC PAYE & NI and Pension Contributions are reconciled as monthly company-wide totals, since those are paid in one lump sum a month with no per-employee trace in the ledger.

Control Accounts

Brought forward + net movement = carried forward, cross-checked to the Trial Balance, for every control account (debtors, creditors, VAT, PAYE, directors' loan, and any other current asset/liability code with nominal activity against it) — with the full nominal-activity postings behind each account's movement, an aged-listing tie-out or a net-movement-by-contact breakdown depending on what applies, and a scan for postings that may have bypassed the right control account entirely.

Uses the Trial Balance / Nominal Activity / Aged Debtors / Aged Creditors sections below — no separate upload here. Runs independently of Generate, so you can test and refine those inputs on their own before building the full working paper.

Fixed Asset Register

Cost/depreciation/NBV rolled forward by category straight from the Trial Balance's own Fixed Asset accounts, plus a possible-capex-miscoded-elsewhere scan — both need only the Trial Balance and Nominal Activity below. The asset-level detail (each asset depreciated by its own method/rate, new additions and possible disposals flagged) additionally needs a prior-year Fixed Asset Register upload; it shows as "n/a" until one is confirmed.

Uses the Trial Balance / Nominal Activity / Fixed Asset Register sections below — no separate upload here. Runs independently of Generate, so you can test and refine those inputs on their own before building the full working paper.
